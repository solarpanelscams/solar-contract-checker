## What It Checks
1. **Dealer Fee Markup**: Compares cash price per watt ($/W) against financed balance to detect 20%-35% hidden origination fees.
2. **Payment Escalation (Month 18 Balloon)**: Flags clauses requiring turnover of federal Investment Tax Credits (ITC).
3. **UCC-1 Fixture Filings**: Detects personal property and fixture liens placed on property deeds.
4. **FTC Holder Rule Protections**: Cites 16 C.F.R. § 433 lender liability for solar installer misrepresentations.
